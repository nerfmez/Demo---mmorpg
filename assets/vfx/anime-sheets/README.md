# Anime fire and smoke sheets (trial)

`anime_fire_3x2.png` (birth, grow, peak, break-up, embers) and `anime_smoke_3x2.png` (6 frames) are
from the stand of `rng-vfx` by Maks (rengi.dev), https://github.com/MRRengi/rng-vfx, under the MIT
licence in `LICENSE-rng-vfx.txt`. The owner picked this anime look as the quality reference
(2026-10-26) and asked to try the effects with these images first; they may be replaced by images
the owner supplies. Cells are 320 px, played stepped at 12 drawings per second.
