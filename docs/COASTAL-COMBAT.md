# Coastal monster attack patterns

Regular Azure Coast creatures use different rhythms instead of sharing a charge.
The authoritative tuning is `data/monsters.json`; monster/save/drop IDs are unchanged.

| Creature | Pattern | Player response |
|---|---|---|
| Salt slime | Walk in, draw back and slap while planted; occasionally spit a slow salt glob at mid range. | Step out of the frontal slap or sideways after the projectile aim locks. |
| Shore gull | Walk in, raise its beak, peck, then walk away and pause before approaching again. | Dodge the narrow peck; catch it during recovery or follow the retreat. |
| Reef crab | Open and raise its claws, then pinch slowly in front. No shell cycle. | Circle to its side after aim locks and punish the long recovery. |
| Hermit crab | Heavier pinch; shell after two attacks when ready, or after four hits in two seconds. | Wait out shell, then punish its 1.1-second emergence (35% more incoming damage). |

Every contact attack has anticipation, contact and follow-through. `hitTime` is
measured from the end of windup; each strike tests its locked frontal arc once.
The existing body radii contribute to reach. The warning sector shows the attack's
reach from the monster centre; a target's collision radius can overlap its edge.
Walking/chasing/retreating causes no contact damage. Slime projectiles use ordinary
terrain collision and deal no poison damage. A blocked gull retreat ends after its
configured duration. Shell reduction ends before hermit emergence starts and never
persists after a leash return.

The renderer reads the simulation's action times. Salt slime squashes and reaches,
gull uses its neck/beak, crabs open/reach/close claws, and hermit tucks head/claws/legs
under its shell. New rigs and telegraphs retain the existing pooling/disposal rules.
Imported art, other species, bosses, player targeting and save version stay intact.
