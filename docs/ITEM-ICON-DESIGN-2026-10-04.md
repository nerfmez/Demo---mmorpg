# Expanded item visual authority v1

Canon: approved existing 512px item illustrations. This brief defines shared garment construction for icons and future modular 3D; it does not create classes, equipment restrictions, complete sets, new items, or grade variants. C/B/A/S and other grades share the same item-ID image/model. Weapons are independently designed with unrestricted silhouette variety within their catalog weapon type and identity.

## Shared icon / future model contract

- Each worn item ID references one family and controlled local deltas. Design sketches/model guides should record neckline, shoulder envelope, hem length, wrist/ankle opening and material boundaries from these illustrated silhouettes.
- Torso raised parts attach to chestWear/chest; glove cuffs and back-of-hand pieces follow the hands; layered shoulders must clear upper-arm rotation. Existing renderer capability is not proof of final fit or deformation: future 3D work must inspect front/side/back and normal-speed motion.
- Do not change approved icon colors merely to match current generic renderer branches. Current shared `beetle` and `hood` branches collapse multiple distinct IDs; future mapping should distinguish them.
- Existing tiers are item levels 1/6/11/16/21. They are not complete outfits in every family. Heavy/agile/magic can describe construction; they are not new game classes.
- Individual weapons keep independent shapes: jagged shell cleaver, wavy kris, moonleaf wand, lantern wand, asymmetric storm bow and so on. Garment-template limits do not apply to them.

## Icon contract

Clean dimensional anime fantasy illustration, fine warm contour lines, cel-shaded facets, upper-left highlights, true transparent background. Full isolated object, no scenery, text, borders, rarity labels or human body. Boots/gloves may be paired. 512x512 RGBA PNG, 16px safe outer margin, item-ID filename; preserve old file bytes. Review large previews and 40px inventory representations on dark and light UI backgrounds. Native generated sheets retained separately; extracted artwork may have less than 512px native detail and is fitted to the existing deployment size without claiming native 512 generation.
